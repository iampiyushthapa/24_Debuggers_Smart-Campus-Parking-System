# 24_Debuggers_Smart-Campus-Parking-System

The Smart Campus Parking System is a C program designed to manage vehicles in a parking area.

Requirements filled:
•	Maintain a fixed number of parking spaces.
•	Record vehicle registration numbers and entry times or simplified parking durations.
•	Calculate parking charges based on duration.
•	Prevent duplicate entry and report when the parking area is full.

output: Parking availability, vehicle status, and parking fee.
